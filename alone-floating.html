alone, floating

‘alone, floating’ is my second animated short film made using my image-to-text AI program to create AI influenced animation. In this film, I used AI to deepen textures, and add life to the color, lighting, and stylization to a 3D CGI animation.

This base of this animated short film used a 3D CGI animation I made. Field, sky, body, mind used the same process with footage shot in real life.
